# Golden Loop

## Overview

**Brand & Style.** Golden Loop (金黄灿烂) is the visual language for a single-page, scene-based personal homepage: a sunny, anime-cute (动漫可爱) re-skin of the reference scene-scroll portfolio. The reference's *structure* is kept exactly — a full-bleed illustrated entry cover, a large hero carrying the person's name, a fanned project-card stack, a project detail overlay, a scrollable index scene, a dated hobby-photo carousel with a detail overlay, a contact block with three channel rows, a local-only feedback form, and a persistent scene indicator — but its **dark cinematic tone stays replaced** and its earlier four-hue pastel skin is now retired. The whole site reads as one warm gold, amber and honey family.

**Personality.** Golden, warm, handmade, a little playful: a sunlit sticker journal rather than a game UI. Chunky rounded shapes, generous whitespace, honey-tinted soft shadows, harmless sparkles and ribbons. The emotional target is "温暖、明亮、让人愿意多停留一会儿" while remaining calm and clearly readable for adult visitors, recruiters and collaborators.

**Visual non-negotiables.**

- Light mode only. Page background is a soft pale cream (`#FFFBEF`), never black, and never pure white as a full-bleed surface.
- The palette is a **single warm hue family — gold, amber and honey** — stepped into four band grounds by lightness and temperature rather than by hue. There is no blue-led, green-led or purple-led value anywhere (the one `success` token is a deep olive semantic state, never a palette lead), and there is no pink or rose at all. Cool greys are replaced by warm creams.
- Every text colour is a deep warm brown / dark amber ink. Gold, amber and honey values are **fills, borders and illustration only** and never carry text.
- Every interactive element is a capsule or a chunky rounded blob; there are no sharp corners anywhere in the system.
- Motion is gentle and springy (bounce, wiggle, float, twinkle); no harsh cuts, no high-contrast flashes, no autoplaying loops that fight reading.
- Decoration is always `aria-hidden`, always behind or beside text, never behind body copy at more than 0.25 opacity, and never inside the reading column at all. Text never sits directly on the cover illustration without its wash or glass layer.
- Chinese-first typography with small uppercase English accent labels; latin display accent allowed for scene titles (`Projects`, `Contact`) exactly as the reference mixes them.

## Colors

The palette is now a **single warm family — gold, amber and honey** — with no second hue. Blue, green, purple and pink are gone: there is no blue-led, green-led or purple-led value anywhere (the single `success` token is a deep olive used only for the success state), and no pink or rose anywhere. Cool greys are replaced by warm creams. The system stays legible on a hue this low in contrast because **every text colour is a deep warm brown / dark amber ink**, and every gold, amber or honey value is a fill, border or illustration only.

- **Surface / Pale Cream (#FFFBEF):** The page canvas and the lightest full-bleed ground. A warm cream that keeps the whole page sunny without glare. It is not pure white and never a cool grey.
- **Surface Alt / Soft Wheat (#F9F2E0), Surface Mint / Warm Honey Wash (#F8E9C4), Surface Sky / Deeper Amber Wash (#F0DCA8):** The three alternating band tints, all in the same warm family and separated by **lightness and temperature steps** instead of by hue. The legacy token key names are kept for implementation stability; each now holds a warm gold step. Bands use `blob`-radius top/bottom or a scalloped decorative edge so they feel soft.
- **Surface Card (#FFFFFF) and Surface Sunken (#F7EDD6):** Cards stay a warm-neutral white so tinted grounds read through the gutters; the sunken warm sand backs index-row hover and the custom scrollbar track.
- **Band assignment (new scene order).** **01 欢迎** sits on `surface` pale cream *behind the full-bleed cover illustration* (visible only as the fallback/poster colour and at the image's cropped edges); **02 目录** = `surface-mint` Warm Honey Wash; **03 作品** = `surface-sky` Deeper Amber Wash; **04 爱好** = `surface-alt` Soft Wheat; **05 联系我** = `surface` pale cream (a non-adjacent repeat of scene 01). Adjacent bands stay distinguishable: cream → honey → deeper amber descend in lightness in clear steps, the deeper amber → soft wheat jump is the largest step in the loop, and wheat → cream separates again; where two warm grounds are close, the scalloped edge and the shadow step carry the change as well.
- **On-surface ink / Deep Walnut (#3B2A15):** The single text colour for headings and body on every band and card. **13.7:1 on `surface-card`, 13.3:1 on `surface`, 12.3:1 on `surface-alt`, 11.4:1 on `surface-mint`, 10.2:1 on `surface-sky`.**
- **On-surface soft / Deep Amber Brown (#6B4E24):** Secondary copy, card descriptions, captions under images, scene-indicator text. **7.7:1 on `surface-card`, 7.4:1 on `surface`, 6.8:1 on `surface-alt`, 6.4:1 on `surface-mint`, 5.7:1 on the deepest band `surface-sky`.**
- **On-surface mute / Muted Bronze (#755A2F):** Dates, counters, field metadata and list-row English labels — the lightest text allowed for normal-size text. **6.4:1 on `surface-card`, 6.2:1 on `surface`, 5.8:1 on `surface-alt`, 5.4:1 on `surface-mint`, 4.8:1 on the deepest band `surface-sky`** — so it clears 4.5:1 on every ground in the system.
- **Primary / Golden Amber (#E8A62B) with Primary strong / Deep Amber (#7A4B0F):** The lead gold. `#E8A62B` is fill, border, progress dot and illustration only and **never carries text**; whenever a gold fill must hold text it uses the deep amber `primary-strong` as the ink or as a solid fill with white text — **white label on `primary-strong` = 7.4:1**, `primary-strong` as text on `primary-tint` = **6.5:1**, and `primary-strong` as text on white = **7.4:1**. `primary-soft` (#F3D98B) is the scroll-cue capsule and inactive-dot fill; `primary-tint` (#FCEFCE) is the selected-row ground and tag-pill ground.
- **Secondary / Honey Amber (#D98A2B) with Secondary strong / Burnt Honey (#8A4A12):** Success-adjacent states, "available for collaboration" pills, the checked tag variant, the selected-row tint's sibling, active-card outline. **`secondary-strong` on white = 6.9:1, on `secondary-tint` = 6.0:1**; `#D98A2B` and `secondary-soft` (#F0C37A) are fill and border only.
- **Tertiary / Muted Ochre (#C79A4E) with Tertiary strong / Bronze (#5F430F):** Links inside long copy, the third tag variant, informational chips, and the small uppercase English micro-labels. **`tertiary-strong` on white = 9.2:1, on `tertiary-tint` = 7.7:1**; `#C79A4E` and `tertiary-soft` (#E6CE96) are fill and border only.
- **Accents (#FFCE4A sparkle, #C08A45 ribbon, #F7BE86 apricot-peach):** Sparkles, ribbons, washi tape, the heart bullet, highlight markers and illustration props only. They are pulled onto the gold family yet kept **distinguishable from the lead gold** so they still read as decoration: the sparkle `accent-star` is a brighter, lighter yellow than `primary`, the ribbon `accent-ribbon` is a deeper bronze, and `accent-peach` is a soft warm apricot. They never carry meaning and never sit under text.
- **Success (#4E6B12, 6.1:1 on white) and Error (#B3261E, 6.5:1 on white):** Form validation text and inline state messages, always accompanied by an icon and a text label — never colour alone. `success` is a deep warm olive and is used *only* as a semantic state, never as a palette lead.
- **Borders:** `#EDDFC0` is the default 2px warm hairline; `#DCC58F` is the hover/pressed border, the dotted-rule colour and the divider between stacked list rows. Both sit at ≥3:1 against adjacent surfaces where they carry a boundary.
- **Scrim (#4A3514 at 45%):** A warm dark-amber scrim that backs overlays and the entry cover, always combined with a blur so the golden surfaces behind it stay soft rather than black.
- **Focus ring:** the deep amber `#7A4B0F` (**7.4:1** against white and against every band, and well above 3:1 over the washed cover illustration), always drawn with the 2px white halo so the ring stays visible on any ground.

Every text-on-pair is stated against the **lightest surface it can sit on** (`surface-card`, i.e. pure white — the highest-contrast case) **and** against the **deepest warm band it can sit on** (`surface-sky`, the lowest-contrast case), so a pair that clears the deepest band clears every ground between. All normal-size text reaches at least **4.5:1** and all large display text reaches at least **3:1** (the display faces at 56–64px clear 10:1 on every ground). The gold, amber and honey base values and every `-soft` / `-tint` value are fills, borders and illustration only — text always uses `on-surface`, `on-surface-soft`, `on-surface-mute`, or a `-strong` variant.

## Typography

A Chinese-first pairing: a chunky rounded Latin display face for the soft anime feel, and a clean rounded body face for long Chinese runs. The type system is unchanged from the earlier skin — only the ink is now deep warm brown instead of navy-violet.

- **Display / headings — `Baloo 2`** (Latin, rounded, chunky) with **`ZCOOL KuaiLe`** allowed as the CJK display accent for the hero and scene titles only, falling back to **`Noto Sans SC` / `PingFang SC`**. Used for the hero line, scene headings (目录, 项目作品, 爱好展示, 联系我), overlay titles, card titles and panel titles. Weight 700, tight track only on Latin (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`); Chinese glyphs never get negative tracking beyond `-0.02em`.
- **Body — `Nunito`** with `Noto Sans SC` / `PingFang SC` fallback. Weight 400 only; the warm voice comes from the rounded terminals, not from heavy weights. Long Chinese paragraphs use `body-md` (16px / 1.7) and never exceed 34 Chinese characters per line in the desktop content column.
- **Micro-labels — `Quicksand`** uppercase with wide tracking (0.16–0.18em) is the reference's signature device and is kept: `PROJECTS`, `INDEX`, `GALLERY`, `PROFILE`, `SCENE 02 · 目录`, `EMAIL`, `STATUS`. On mobile, tracking stays but font size is never reduced below 11px.
- **Rules:** at most two weights per screen (400 + 700, or 400 + 800 in the contact block). Numbers in counters, dates and status labels use `tabular-nums`. Every heading is an HTML heading; every English micro-label is decorative text (`span`/`p`), never a heading. The index list's Chinese row labels are the one exception to the two-weight rule: they carry 500 at rest and 700 when selected, inside an already-selected context.

## Layout

**Scene model.** The page is one uninterrupted scroll of five full-viewport scenes, in this order: **01 欢迎 → 02 目录 → 03 作品 → 04 爱好 → 05 联系我**. Chrome is exactly as the reference: a fixed header (wordmark left, control pills right) whose scene-progress pill carries **five dots, one per scene in the new order**; a fixed bottom scene-indicator row reading `SCENE 01 · 欢迎` … `SCENE 05 · 联系我`; and a scene content region padded `scene-pad-top` (108px) top and `scene-pad-bottom` (96px) bottom with `gutter` (72px) sides. Overlays (entry cover, project detail, gallery detail) sit above everything on the `scrim`.

**Band rhythm.** Scenes alternate across the four warm band grounds so each scene change reads as a lightness/temperature change before any shadow is used: **01 欢迎 = `surface`** (pale cream, behind the full-bleed cover image), **02 目录 = `surface-mint`** (warm honey wash), **03 作品 = `surface-sky`** (deeper amber wash), **04 爱好 = `surface-alt`** (soft wheat), **05 联系我 = `surface`** (pale cream, non-adjacent repeat). Because 目录 is now the second band, the 目录 index panel sits on the honey-wash ground and is never white-on-white; because 作品 is the third band, the fanned cards sit on the deeper amber wash so their white frames pop.

**Content grid.** Desktop scene split is **38 / 62**: a narrow left "narrative" column (eyebrow, large heading, one-line subtitle, or the contact intro) and a wide right "content" column (fanned card stack, index list, carousel, profile panel, form). The two columns are separated by a `2xl` (48px) gap; the whole scene is capped at `container-max` (1200px) and centred, so on very wide screens the whitespace grows outside the content, never inside. The 目录 scene uses the same split, with the scrollable index panel as its content column.

**Spacing rhythm.** A strict 8px base with a 4px half-step (`spacing` scale). Scene sections use `3xl`–`4xl` vertical rhythm; component internals use `sm`–`lg`. Related items share a container (card, glass panel, blob band) with `xl` internal padding so grouping reads as containment rather than as whitespace.

**Shapes of the scene archetypes.**

- *Cover scene (欢迎):* one full-bleed illustration filling the whole first viewport edge to edge (including behind the fixed header); the entry-gate cluster (wordmark top-left, centred entry capsule) and the hero cluster (narrative bottom-left, glass profile panel bottom-right) are composed over that one image and one wash so the cover reads as a single continuous surface.
- *Index scene (目录):* the 38/62 skeleton with the narrative block — `eyebrow` (`INDEX`) → Chinese scene heading (目录) in `scene-heading` → one-line subtitle in `body-lg` → one short help line in `caption` — and the scrollable index panel filling the content column.
- *Fan scene (作品):* heading block top-left of the narrative column, the fanned bounce-card stack centred in the right column, both vertically centred.
- *List scene (爱好):* the same skeleton, with the horizontal carousel filling the right column.
- *Contact scene (联系我):* intro in the narrative column, channel rows and the feedback panel across the content area.

**Responsive behaviour.**

- **≥1200px — Desktop.** Full 38/62 split, hero at 64px, the cover illustration at one full viewport, the fanned container 640×430px with `card-gap` (24px) to its neighbours, the 目录 index panel capped at 400px tall with 12px internal padding, carousel shows 1 full slide + 2 peeks of ~12% each, contact block is 2 columns (intro + channel rows left, feedback panel right), overlays are centred panels (max 1080px wide, media 44% / content 56%).
- **768–1199px — Tablet.** `gutter-tablet` (40px) sides; the scene keeps two columns but narrows the narrative column to 34%; scene headings drop to 40px; the fanned container shrinks to 520×360px; the index panel caps at 380px; carousel shows 1 slide + one 12% peek; overlays become single-column and scrollable with the media on top.
- **375–767px — Mobile.** Everything stacks in source order: header (compact — wordmark may collapse to its dot mark), scene indicator row pinned at the bottom, content region padded `gutter-mobile` (20px) with `scene-pad-top` reduced to 72px and bottom to 88px so the pinned indicator never overlaps content. The narrative column becomes a full-width heading block (eyebrow → heading → subtitle → decorative dotted rule). The fanned container becomes 340×260px and shows only the stack. The 目录 index list goes full width of the content column, its height cap drops to 320px and its row padding tightens to 10px / 12px, but the row tap target stays ≥44px (48px) and the fade masks are kept. The gallery carousel shows one slide + a 10% peek of the next, swipeable, with the prev/next controls and the counter on one row beneath the track. Contact rows stack full-width. The feedback form stacks full-width with the submit button full-width (min 48px tall). Overlays become full-screen sheets: `rounded.xl` only on the top corners, internal padding 20–24px, the whole sheet scrollable, and a sticky 44px close button. Decorative float/wiggle/twinkle animation is disabled; only 180ms opacity fades remain.
- **Never:** horizontal overflow at any width. Long words, contact values and long project titles wrap or break-word; images are `object-fit: cover` inside fixed-ratio frames; the carousel track uses `overflow: hidden` and never widens the page; the fanned card stack is scaled so its widest transform stays inside its own container at every breakpoint (including a 390px viewport).

**Layering.** The cover image layer and scene backgrounds/bands (`z-index 0`) → decorations (`1`) → scene content (`2`) → fixed header and scene indicator (`40`) → carousel controls and the index panel's fade masks (`5`, inside their section) → overlay scrim (`60`) → overlay panel and its close button (`61`). The fixed header and scene indicator must always be readable over the cover: they sit on `surface-overlay` glass pills rather than directly on the illustration.

**Touch targets.** Every tappable element — buttons, tags that act as filters, carousel controls, close buttons, index rows, bounce-cards, contact rows that link out, form fields — is at least **44 × 44 CSS px** on every breakpoint, including the mobile pill navigation. Where a control looks smaller than 44px, it gets an invisible padded hit area rather than a smaller target.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is soft and **warm**, never cinematic. Hierarchy comes from four cooperating devices:

1. **Warm banding.** Scenes alternate between `surface` cream, `surface-mint` honey wash, `surface-sky` deeper amber wash and `surface-alt` soft wheat so sections separate by lightness and temperature before any shadow is used.
2. **White cards on warm ground.** Primary content sits on `surface-card` white with a 2px `border` hairline; the tinted ground shows through the gutters.
3. **Warm amber shadows.** Shadows are tinted to the gold family (warm amber, never cool blue-violet and never pink), low-opacity and large: `0 2px 8px rgba(176, 118, 18, 0.16)`, `0 8px 24px rgba(176, 118, 18, 0.20)`, `0 18px 44px rgba(158, 102, 12, 0.22)`, `0 28px 72px rgba(134, 84, 8, 0.24)`. Card rest = level 2; card hover = level 3; overlay panel, entry capsule and the index panel = level 2; the hero glass panel = level 4. No element ever stacks more than two shadow levels.
4. **Sticker lift.** Decorative badges, tags and washi tape add a small hard offset shadow (`0 4px 0 rgba(176, 118, 18, 0.35)`) plus a white 4px ring so they read as peelable stickers rather than as elevated UI.

**Glass.** Translucent surfaces are light, not dark: `surface-overlay` (warm white at **82%**) with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(1.1)`, used for the fixed header pills, the hero profile panel, the entry capsule, the gallery caption sticker and the scene indicator. The 82% value (up from the earlier 78%) and the 2px white border are what keep text legible where glass sits over the cover illustration; behind a photo or a strongly coloured illustration the 2px white border is mandatory so the edge stays visible.

**Cover legibility (scene 01).** The cover illustration is an extremely light, low-contrast pale-gold pattern, so legibility is secured by a **warm wash** and by glass rather than by the image itself. A single vertical gradient wash of `rgba(255, 251, 239, …)` sits over the whole image: **18% at the top, 34% at the 46% mark, and 68% at the bottom**, plus a soft cream edge vignette. Against that washed image the headline pair measures **`on-surface` ≥ 11:1** and the support line **`on-surface-soft` ≥ 6.4:1**; because micro-labels would drop to ≈4.5:1 over the darkest pattern strokes in the palest top third, **scene-01 micro-labels use `on-surface-soft` (≥6.4:1) rather than `on-surface-mute`**, and no small `on-surface-mute` text is placed in the top third of the cover. Headline (≥11:1) and support line (≥6.4:1) both clear their 4.5:1 floor, and the 64px display headline clears its 3:1 large-text target by a wide margin. The wordmark sits on the washed image (deep ink, ≥11:1); the entry capsule and the profile panel sit on 82% glass over the washed image, so their labels measure **≥11:1** and their 2px white borders keep the glass edge visible.

**Overlay depth.** Detail overlays use `scrim` plus `blur(8px)` on the layer below. The overlay panel itself is opaque `surface-card` white with `rounded.xl`, so text contrast never depends on what is behind it. The entry cover is the one exception: it is a full-bleed illustrated scene, so the entry capsule must itself be a solid-enough glass capsule (82% warm white) to keep its own label legible.

## Shapes

The shape language is **chunky soft**: nothing is sharp, nothing is thin.

- **Radius scale mapping.** `xs 10px` small chips, inset thumbs and code-ish accents; `sm 14px` tag pills that must stay compact and checkbox/radio wells; `md 20px` inputs, textareas, gallery caption stickers, index rows; `lg 28px` info cards inside overlays, bounce-card frames and covers, the alternating band panels and the index panel; `xl 36px` the fanned stack container, glass panels, contact rows, overlay panels and mobile sheet tops; `blob 48px` the hero profile panel, feedback panel and decorative blobs; `full 999px` every button, tag acting as a filter, carousel control, close button, progress dot, index badge and the entry capsule.
- **Blob / cloud panels.** Large panels combine `blob` radius with one asymmetric corner to break the rectangle: `border-radius: 48px 48px 48px 16px` for a "speech bubble" panel, or `48px 48px 16px 48px` when it sits on the right. Use at most one asymmetric panel per viewport.
- **Cloud edges.** Band sections may use a decorative scalloped top or bottom edge (a repeating 24px-radius half-circle mask in the same colour as the band) to signal a scene change; decorative only, `aria-hidden`, and never between a heading and its own body copy.
- **Dotted and dashed accents.** 2px dotted rules (`border-top: 2px dotted #DCC58F`) replace the reference's thin hairline under scene micro-labels; 2px dashed outlines are reserved for "empty slot" states such as an unfilled gallery item or a drop-zone-style note. Dashes never carry the only meaning of a state — a text label always accompanies them.
- **Sticker badge.** Decorative motif held by a white 4px ring + hard offset shadow, optionally rotated −6° to +6°, maximum 3 per viewport, `aria-hidden`.
- **Index notch.** The index list's selection indicator is a 4px × 20px `rounded.full` capsule of `primary-strong` — a short rounded shape, never a full-height square left border. It is an interaction state, not a card decoration.
- **Bounce-card frame.** Each fanned card is a white `lg`-radius frame with a 6px warm-white inner ring around its 16:10 cover image, so the four cards read as a stack of polaroids rather than as bare photos. The frame radius and ring are what make the fan legible where cards overlap.
- **Icons.** Rounded stroke icons: 2px stroke, round caps and joins, 24px grid, 44px hit area; the same rounded geometry as the type. Solid glyphs are used only for tiny heart/star/sparkle decorations.
- **Images.** Bounce-card covers are 16:10 with `rounded.lg` and the white inner ring; gallery slides are 4:3 (desktop) and 3:2 (mobile) with `rounded.lg` and a 2px white inner border; overlay media keeps the source ratio with `rounded.lg` on the inner corners only. Placeholder imagery is a soft two-stop warm gradient (`primary-tint → secondary-tint`, pale gold → pale amber) so an unpopulated slot still looks intentional.
- **No** sharp-cornered panels, no 1px-only crisp borders, no square avatars, no hard drop shadows in black.

### Cover scene (01 欢迎) — full-bleed image

Scene 01 is a single full-bleed cover built from **one user-supplied portrait illustration** (a very pale golden pattern of stylised cat faces, roughly 1371 × 1920, aspect ≈ 0.714). The entry-gate cluster and the hero cluster are both composed over that one image and one wash, so the cover reads as one continuous surface rather than as two stacked panels.

- **Image layer.** An absolutely positioned layer (`inset: 0`) with `object-fit: cover` / `background-size: cover`, `object-position: center` / `background-position: center`, `no-repeat`. The image **fills the whole first viewport edge to edge, including behind the fixed header**; it preserves its aspect ratio, is **cropped rather than stretched, centred, never tiled, never distorted**. The scene is `position: relative; min-height: 100svh` (it may grow a little taller on mobile so the stacked content fits, and the image layer still covers the whole scene element).
- **How much of the illustration shows.** The source is portrait, so the crop changes direction by breakpoint: **≥1200px** — the viewport is much wider than the image, so `cover` scales to the width and crops top and bottom, leaving the full width and roughly the **central 45% of the illustration's height** (e.g. a 1440 × 900 viewport). **768–1199px** — still wider than the image, so it crops top and bottom again, leaving the full width and roughly the **central 54% of the height** (e.g. 1024 × 768). **≤767px** — the viewport is now more portrait than the image, so `cover` switches to cropping the sides: the **full height shows and roughly the central 65% of the width** (a 390px-wide viewport shows the central ≈255px of the 1371px-wide file, scaled). The crop is always centred, so the cat-face pattern and its symmetry stay intact.
- **Wash layer.** A single vertical gradient wash of `rgba(255, 251, 239, …)` sits above the image and below all content: **18% opacity at the top, 34% at the 46% mark, 68% at the bottom**, plus a soft cream edge vignette. It is the "wash layer" that makes ink legible; text never sits on the raw illustration.
- **Entry-gate cluster.** Wordmark top-left (a 10px `primary` dot plus the name in `eyebrow`-tracked uppercase, set on the washed image, **≥11:1**), and one centred **entry capsule**: two stacked lines inside one `rounded.full` pill — the Chinese line in `panel-title` and the English line in `label-en` beneath it. Hover = spring lift +4px with a sparkle appearing at its top-right corner, active = 2px press, focus = 3px `focus-ring` (`#7A4B0F`, 7.4:1) with a 2px white halo. It is the first focusable element; `Esc` is not required.

Its ink measures **≥11:1** on the glass.
- **Motif re-judgement over the image.** Because the illustration is itself a repeating soft pattern, the hero's decoration is thinned so it does not fight it: the **sparkles stay** (4-point stars, `accent-star` at **0.45 opacity**), placed in the top-left and bottom-right outer margins only, never inside a text zone; the **ribbon stays** pinned to the profile panel's top-right corner at −8° (it sits on glass, not on the illustration); the **blush dots stay** at the profile panel's bottom-left (also on glass). The **petal drift is dropped on scene 01 entirely** — its soft-ellipse vocabulary duplicates the cat pattern and muddies it; petals are used instead in the outer margins of scenes without a patterned image. The **chibi sticker is not used on scene 01** (its silhouette would compete with the pattern); it appears once on scene 04. No other motif is placed on the cover, and **no text is ever placed on the raw illustration without the wash (or glass) layer**.

### Scene 作品 — fanned "BounceCards" stack

The scene's four project images are no longer a 2×2 grid: they are a **fanned stack of four cards** that animates in with an elastic bounce. Each card holds one 16:10 cover image. The stack is built with the `gsap` library on entrance — `gsap.fromTo('.card', { scale: 0 }, { scale: 1, stagger: 0.08, ease: 'elastic.out(1, 0.5)', delay: 1 })` — and the fan also drives hover, focus and reset. The section heading block and the rest of the scene skeleton are unchanged; only the image display changes.

- **Container.** `bounce-card-container`: a fixed-size `position: relative` container centred in the scene's content column, holding **four absolutely positioned cards**. Size by breakpoint — **≥1200px: 640 × 430px**; **768–1199px: 520 × 360px**; **≤767px: 340 × 260px** (i.e. the full content column inside the 20px mobile gutters). The container is the overflow containment box for the fan.
- **Cards.** `bounce-card`: `surface-card` white frame, `rounded.lg` (28px), a **6px warm-white inner ring** (`padding: 6px`) around the cover, and the level-2 warm amber shadow at rest. Inside the frame each card holds **one 16:10 cover** (`object-fit: cover`, `rounded.lg`, 2px white inner ring). Card size by breakpoint — **≥1200px: 272 × 170px**; **768–1199px: 220 × 138px**; **≤767px: 160 × 100px**. The four cards are absolutely positioned at the container's centre and spread only by their transforms.
- **Resting transforms (rotation + horizontal translate).** **≥1200px** — card 1 `rotate(-14deg) translateX(-150px)`, card 2 `rotate(-5deg) translateX(-50px)`, card 3 `rotate(5deg) translateX(50px)`, card 4 `rotate(14deg) translateX(150px)`. **768–1199px** — card 1 `rotate(-13deg) translateX(-120px)`, card 2 `rotate(-4deg) translateX(-40px)`, card 3 `rotate(4deg) translateX(40px)`, card 4 `rotate(13deg) translateX(120px)`. **≤767px** — card 1 `rotate(-12deg) translateX(-72px)`, card 2 `rotate(-4deg) translateX(-24px)`, card 3 `rotate(4deg) translateX(24px)`, card 4 `rotate(12deg) translateX(72px)`. Transforms originate from each card's own centre.
- **Resting z-index order.** Back-to-front left-to-right: card 1 = `1`, card 2 = `2`, card 3 = `3`, card 4 = `4` (later cards overlap earlier ones). The hovered/focused card raises to `z-index: 10`. Only one card is raised and only one is selected at a time.
- **Fan fits, never overflows.** At **≥1200px** a ±14° card is ≈305px wide, so `translateX(150) + 152.5 = 302.5` per side → ≈605px inside the 640px container; at **768–1199px** a ±13° card is ≈245px wide, so `120 + 122.7 = 242.7` per side → ≈485px inside 520px; at **≤767px** a ±12° card is ≈177px wide, so `72 + 88.6 = 160.6` per side → ≈321px inside 340px, which fits a **390px-wide viewport** inside the 20px gutters (350px usable). The fan therefore stays inside its container at every breakpoint and produces **no horizontal page overflow**.
- **Hover / focus.** When a card is hovered or focused: the card itself straightens — `rotate(0deg)`, keeping its own resting `translateX` — while its **neighbours push outward** approximately **±160px at desktop**, **±130px at tablet** and **±80px at mobile**, animated with `back.out(1.4)` over **0.4s**, each neighbour delayed by **0.05s per step of distance** (immediate neighbour 0.05s, next 0.10s, and so on). The transform animates on a spring curve; the shadow step and colour change use the curve easing. The stack **resets** to its resting transforms on `mouseleave` (0.4s `back.out(1.4)`), and on blur for keyboard users.
- **Focus-visible.** The existing focus treatment is kept on every card — a 3px `focus-ring` (`#7A4B0F`, 7.4:1) with a 2px white halo drawn around the white frame. **Focus produces the same effect as hover** (straighten + neighbour push + level-3 shadow), so keyboard users see the identical affordance that pointer users do; the halo guarantees the ring stays visible over the deeper amber band.
- **States.** **Rest** = level-2 shadow, resting rotation/translate, white frame. **Hover** = straighten, neighbours out, level-3 shadow, frame border → `border-strong`. **Focus** = same as hover plus the 3px ring + 2px halo. **Active (press)** = a 2px downward press (`translateY(2px)`) with no shadow change, held for the duration of the press. **Selected** (the card whose project detail overlay is open) = the existing selected-outline treatment, a 2px `secondary` (`#D98A2B`) outline around the frame, held until the overlay closes. Only one card is selected at a time.
- **Title treatment.** The cards are **image-only with an accessible label**: a card carries no visible Chinese title, and the project title is **not** revealed on the card. Each card is a `<button>` with an `aria-label` equal to the project's Chinese title, so the accessible name is always present and is announced to assistive tech; the title is also shown in `detail-title` once its overlay opens. This keeps the fan readable as pure imagery and honours the no-text-on-imagery rule.
- **Interaction.** Each card is a button-like element that **opens the existing project detail overlay** on click, `Enter` or `Space` (native `<button>` semantics, so all three work without custom key handling). The overlay, its focus trap, `Esc`-to-close and scroll lock are unchanged; the card that opened it keeps the selected outline until the overlay closes.
- **One-line hint.** The scene's single hint ("hover to spread the cards, click to open the detail") is placed in the **existing heading block's help-line slot** in the `caption` typography role — no new element, no visible title on the cards.
- **Motion fallback.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the stack appears **without the elastic scale bounce**: the cards are rendered at their final resting scale with **a single 180ms opacity fade over the whole stack** (no scale, no stagger, no 1s delay). Hover and focus **do not translate the neighbours and do not change rotation** — only the level-3 shadow, the border change and the focus ring remain, so the affordance is still conveyed without motion.

**Content slots per card:** `cover` (16:10 image), `title` (Chinese, ≤14 chars — carried as the accessible name and in the detail overlay), plus the detail-only fields below.

### Content slots, label patterns and copy tone

- **Copy tone:** short, warm, spoken Chinese sentences (≤20 characters per line where possible), with small uppercase English accents standing in for headings and metadata (`INDEX`, `PROJECTS`, `GALLERY`, `FEEDBACK`, `EMAIL`, `SCENE 02 · 目录`). No exclamation stacking, no marketing superlatives, no emoji inside headings.
- **Bilingual label pattern:** `中文 / English` for form labels, `English / 中文` for scene titles (mirroring the reference's `Contact / 联系我`). Pick one order per surface and never mix both orders in the same block.

### Motion and micro-interactions

- **Durations:** `140ms` micro (hover colour, focus ring, index mask and row-hover fade), `240ms` standard (lift, press, chip toggle), `420ms` expressive (card lift, carousel slide, motif enter), `620ms` scene transition. The BounceCards entrance and the neighbour push use the `gsap` library at **0.4s** with the `elastic.out(1, 0.5)` entrance ease (`stagger: 0.08`, `delay: 1`) and `back.out(1.4)` for hover/focus/reset.
- **Easings:** `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` (spring, default for anything that moves) and `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.9, 0.3, 1)` (soft-out, for opacity and scene cross-fades); GSAP `elastic.out(1, 0.5)` and `back.out(1.4)` are the spring family used for the fanned card stack.
- **Named interactions:** *bounce-in* — the four BounceCards scale `0 → 1` with `elastic.out(1, 0.5)`, 0.08 stagger, 1s delay on scene entry; *spread* — on hover/focus the neighbours push out ±160/±130/±80px while the active card straightens, `back.out(1.4)` over 0.4s, 0.05s stagger per step; *bounce* — hover lift `translateY(-8px) scale(1.02)` on cards, `-4px` on rows; *wiggle* — one-shot `rotate(-2deg) → rotate(2deg) → 0` over 420ms on button hover and on a sticker that becomes active; *float* — hero decorations drift `translateY(-8px)` on a 4s infinite alternate ease-in-out; *twinkle* — sparkle opacity `0.4 → 1` on a 1.8s alternate loop; *press* — `translateY(2px)` with no shadow change; *peek* — the mobile nav pill nudges 4px in the swipe direction; *pop-in* — index rows enter with `scale 0.7 → 1` plus a fade over 200ms (see Scene 目录).
- **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, all float/wiggle/twinkle loops stop, index rows reveal with opacity only (no scale), the BounceCards appear at final scale with a single 180ms opacity fade (no elastic scale, no stagger, no delay) and their hover/focus no longer translates the neighbours or changes rotation, positional transforms collapse to opacity-only fades of 180ms, and the scene transition becomes a 200ms cross-fade. Focus rings and hover colour changes stay, so affordances never disappear.
- Motion is never required to understand state: every animated change is also expressed by colour, border, or text.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the whole page golden and warm: pale cream canvas, warm-white cards, gold / amber / honey bands. Don't reintroduce the reference's dark cinematic gradients, teal-on-black glass, near-black scrims, or any blue, green, purple or pink lead.
- Do keep every gold, amber and honey value as a fill, border or illustration only. Don't put text on gold; where a gold fill needs a label, use the deep amber `primary-strong` as a dark warm ink (white on `primary-strong` = 7.4:1, `primary-strong` on `primary-tint` = 6.5:1).
- Do keep the four warm bands separated by lightness and temperature (cream → honey wash → deeper amber wash → soft wheat), in the new scene order 欢迎, 目录, 作品, 爱好, 联系我. Don't rely on hue to separate scenes.
- Do use only capsule and chunky-rounded shapes; every button, tag, chip, dot, index badge, card frame and control is `rounded.full`, `rounded.lg` or `rounded.xl`.
- Do keep normal-size text at **4.5:1** or better against its actual surface, and large display text at 3:1 or better; every pair in this file is quoted against both the lightest surface (`surface-card`, white) and the deepest band (`surface-sky`, deeper amber), and scene-01 text is quoted against the washed cover image.
- Do keep every tap target at **44 × 44 CSS px** or larger, including carousel controls, dots, index rows, bounce-card frames, close buttons and the mobile prev/next pill.
- Do keep an always-visible focus indicator: 3px `focus-ring` (`#7A4B0F`) with a 2px white halo, and never remove outlines. Focus must produce the same effect as hover, including on the fanned cards.
- Do pair every colour-coded state (error, success, active, selected) with an icon, a shape or a text label; never rely on hue alone — the index row selection uses a notch plus a bolder label, and the selected bounce-card uses a 2px `secondary` outline, not colour alone.
- Do keep the fanned card stack inside its container at every breakpoint so the page never scrolls horizontally — including the 340px mobile container on a 390px viewport.
- Don't animate for its own sake. One playful response per interaction, durations from the 140/240/420/620ms scale plus the 0.4s GSAP stack motion, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks (index rows and BounceCards lose their scale and keep only a fade, and the fan stops translating).
- Do keep decorations behind or beside content, `aria-hidden`, ≤0.25 opacity near copy, capped at three motif types per viewport, and thinned on the cover so they never fight the cat-face illustration.
- Do cap copy to its slot budget and let it wrap; don't let a longer project title, email address, index label or caption resize a card, a row or the carousel.
- Do keep Chinese as the primary voice with small uppercase English accents; don't turn every label bilingual or mix `中文 / English` and `English / 中文` orders inside one block.
- Don't place text directly on photography or illustration. Text always sits on a solid surface, on 82%-opaque warm glass with a 2px white border, or on the cover's cream wash layer — never on the raw cover illustration.
- Do use rounded, 2px-stroke icons and rounded display type; don't introduce sharp-cornered or thin-line icon sets.
- Do preserve the reference's information density and the updated scene order (**欢迎 → 目录 → 作品 → 爱好 → 联系我**); the golden treatment changes the skin and a scene's band ground, never the underlying structure.
